3. What happens after "Send reservation request"

ASuccess
Card is replaced by: "Request received", reference SC-00012, summary, status Pending, a note that a late table is held 15 minutes, button "Make another request".
BMistake in a field, or that time is full
Red message under that field, focus moves to the first wrong field. Nothing is sent.
CServer or database problem
One clear message at the bottom of the card: "Please try again or contact the cafe."

7. Page flow

Customer:  index.html --(fill form, Send)--> success message  (saved as Pending)

Staff:     index.html --(footer "Staff login")--> login.html --(sign in)--> admin.html
           admin.html --(View / Edit / Confirm / Delete)--> changes saved
           admin.html --(Sign out)--> login.html
           anyone without a login who opens admin.html --> sent to login.html
